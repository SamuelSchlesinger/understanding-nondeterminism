# Synthesis and pedagogy

Parent: [research record](../index.md).

The manuscript should teach the following sequence:

1. Quantifiers, gate model, and why hardness is a worst-case question.
2. The truth-table ceiling and what it does and does not imply.
3. Consistency of witness choices; forests, shared vertices, and structural cost.
4. The critical structural balance and its necessary cycle-loss and width conditions.
5. Exact cubic realization and the distinction between supplied and minimum size.
6. Shared restriction circuits and witness coverage as a coupled construction.
7. Input distributions, multiplicity profiles, and necessary hardness conditions.
8. Worked families, conditional examples, prior work, and the remaining hard range.
9. A bounded aggregation direction and the verification methods in appendices.

The integrated manuscript gives complete proofs of the structural rate, the
joint coverage bound, critical structure, exact realization, and analytical examples. The original
polynomial-prefactor candidate is superseded by the stronger structural rate.
The threshold-profile lemma supports the quantitative sparse-fiber population.
Exact literature priority remains unestablished.

The final structural additions focus the open problem. Near-full amplification
at the five-gate threshold would force asymptotic saturation of several bounds.
The realization construction shows that critical gate and cycle budgets can
also be realized by nonminimal verifiers for a simple conjunction. Therefore
acyclicity and those supplied budgets alone do not settle intrinsic hardness;
semantic simplification and restrictions imposed by minimum circuits remain
relevant targets.
