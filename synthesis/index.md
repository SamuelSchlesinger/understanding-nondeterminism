# Synthesis and pedagogy

Parent: [research record](../index.md).

The manuscript should teach the following sequence:

1. Quantifiers, gate model, and why hardness is a worst-case question.
2. The truth-table ceiling and what it does and does not imply.
3. Consistency of witness choices; forests, shared vertices, and structural cost.
4. Shared restriction circuits and witness coverage as a coupled construction.
5. Input distributions, multiplicity profiles, and necessary hardness conditions.
6. Exact comparison with prior structural compilation and circuit lower bounds.
7. Worked families, conditional examples, remaining questions, and proof limits.

The integrated manuscript gives complete proofs of the structural rate, the
joint coverage bound, their consequences, and analytical examples. The original
polynomial-prefactor candidate is superseded by the stronger structural rate.
The threshold-profile lemma supports the quantitative sparse-fiber population.
Exact literature priority remains unestablished.
