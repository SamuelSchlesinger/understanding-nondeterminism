## Findings

### F1: Lialina's POMI original is already in English

- **Location:** `references.bib:202`, entry `Lialina2020`; `research/literature/statement-matrix.md:66`, bibliographic corrections.
- **Severity:** Error, minor bibliography metadata.
- **Finding:** The bibliography calls the 2018 paper a "Russian original", and the matrix contrasts it with the "English publication" in 2020. The original POMI paper is itself in English. [MathNet's original-journal record](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/znsl6688) explicitly records its language as English. The [institutional PDF's indexed first page](https://ftp.pdmi.ras.ru/pub/publicat/znsl/v475/p122.pdf) contains the English title, abstract, and introduction. The [2020 publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10958-020-04813-1) says it was published in POMI 475 (2018), 122-136; it does not call that original Russian-language. The years, pages, author, DOI, and two SAT exponents are otherwise consistent.
- **Fix:** Use "Original publication: Zap. Nauchn. Sem. POMI 475, 122-136 (2018)" in the BibTeX note. In the matrix use "Lialina's original POMI publication is 2018 and its Journal of Mathematical Sciences publication is 2020; the original is already in English." Propagate this correction to the master bibliography during assembly if needed.

### F2: The local Morizumi title drops a word

- **Location:** `research/literature/statement-matrix.md:100`, local reference `morizumi-width`.
- **Severity:** Polish, title transcription.
- **Finding:** The local reference says "Some Results on Circuit Complexity ...". The [arXiv v2 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01347v2) and [full author text](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.01347v2) both include "the" before "Circuit Complexity". The entry in `references.bib` already has the correct title. Author, identifier, v2 date, theorem numbers, and bounds agree.
- **Fix:** Copy the exact title from `references.bib`: "Some Results on the Circuit Complexity of Bounded Width Circuits and Nondeterministic Circuits". Preserve it when assembling `research/sources.md`.
